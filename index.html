Your day

Home is a dashboard of your day.

A brief in a few lines, your mail sorted by what needs an answer, today's tasks, the weather, and the apps you've built, as widgets you place yourself. Replies are drafted for you to approve. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

Your apps

It uses Chrome and your apps, where you can see it.

Ask Kaliko to do something on a site or in an app on your PC. It works in your own Chrome through the Kaliko extension, and in desktop apps like WhatsApp. A frame shows it's at work, and its cursor moves to each thing it clicks.

  • You approveIt asks before it acts, once for each site in a chat.
  • It seesIt reads the page or a screenshot of the window, the way a person looks.
  • It stays outIt never presses sign-in buttons or opens a chat you didn't name.

System intelligence

It knows your machine before you pick a model.

Kaliko reads your graphics card, your memory and each model's real size, then tells you plainly how it will run. Download a model once and Kaliko runs it itself: free and offline. Each download is checked against its published fingerprint.

  • Runs well hereFits in the graphics card's memory. Full speed.
  • Runs, partly in memorySplit between the card and system memory. It works, just slower.
  • Too bigWould page to disk and freeze the PC, so you're told before you click.

Privacy and control

Your models. Your data. Your rules.

01

Private by default

Models, chats and files stay on your computer. Nothing leaves unless you connect a cloud provider yourself.

  • No account, no sign-up
  • Keeps working offline
  • Remembers only what you ask it to
  • Personal details masked before a cloud model sees them
02

You decide how it runs

Choose the model for every chat and the runtime underneath it, and set how much context it gets.

  • Pick a model per chat, or let several answer
  • Set the context window to fit your card
  • Mix local and cloud however you want
03

Nothing happens behind your back

Anything that sends, deletes or changes something stops and asks first. Every change can be undone.

  • Approvals that survive a restart
  • Apps it builds run sandboxed, kept out of Kaliko's data
  • A tamper-evident audit log, every turn traced
